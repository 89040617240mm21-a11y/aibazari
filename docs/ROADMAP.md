# Roadmap · Дорожная карта

## ✅ Done · Сделано (25.10.2026)
- Name and domain **aibazari.com** · Имя и домен
- Own server in Germany, firewall, auto-updates, brute-force protection · Свой сервер в Германии
- Open repository, concept, contribution rules · Открытый репозиторий и концепция

## 🟡 Stage 1 — Telegram bot (weeks 1–2) · Бот
- Sign in via Telegram, language auto-detect
- Post a listing in free text / voice / photo → AI makes a clean card
- Search, "write to the author", my listings, delete
- Directions at start: things, give away, ask for help, services

## ⚪ Stage 2 — Website (weeks 3–4) · Сайт
- aibazari.com: listings searchable by Google, city pages
- Same account as the bot (Telegram login)

## ⚪ Stage 3 — More directions · Новые направления
- Jobs, rides & delivery, learning, travel & leisure
- City/country Telegram channels where the bot posts listings

## ⚪ Stage 4 — Trust & scale · Доверие и рост
- Ratings, scam reports, appeals
- Mirrors in several countries, federation of servers
- Donations page, transparency report (costs, numbers)

Want to change the order? Open a [Discussion](../../../discussions) — the roadmap is public on purpose.
